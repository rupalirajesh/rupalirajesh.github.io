Hi, I'm **Rupali Rajesh**, a Computer Science (and Data Science) student at BITS Goa.

I work on large language models and, more recently, on **how audio-language models
understand music** — where they perceive it correctly, where they don't, and why.
I'm interested in questions that sit between machine learning and perception: what a
model's internal representations actually contain, and how that relates to what it can
say out loud.
